# How it looks and behaves

Material 3, with Google's own simplicity: white surfaces, one blue, Google Sans. Colours, type and shapes are in
`src/theme`.

## The owner's rules

- The keypad has digits, the point and the check, and nothing else. Backspace is beside the amount. (Filling in cents first
  needs no point: there its key deletes.)
- The note is a full pill.
- Filters are in one dropdown.
- Spending is never red. Income is green. Red is for errors, deleting, and being over a budget.
- Tabs and pages switch at once, without a transition. Entry and edit pages slide up.
- The add button is at the bottom right, and tucks away while scrolling down.
- The search bar on Home is 52 high; opening it only widens it.
- Onboarding is four pages at most, with the app's own components as pictures.
- No Google artwork, except the official sign-in button.
- Every status has words or an icon, never colour alone.

## What it isn't

Not a bank app: no bank logins, no payments, no selling. No importing, streaks, feeds, offers or ads. Gold, silver and two
ethical funds are the only assets. Prices come once a day and are in rupees.

## Words

Short and plain, in the second person, no exclamation marks. Say what happened and what to do: "Couldn't save on this
phone. Free up some space." Buttons say what they do: "Erase data", not "OK".

## Everyone can use it

Targets are 48 or more, text grows with the phone's setting (headline amounts a little), each control has a role and a
name, lists have headings, and swiping to delete also has a menu and a spoken action.
